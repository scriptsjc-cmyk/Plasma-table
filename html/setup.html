These are the usable edges. — the area the torch can actually reach. That can be a little smaller than the bed size (the rails' travel, for example 1300 × 800 mm).

  1. Set home (0,0). This table has no limit switches, so it cannot find home by itself. Jog the torch to your marked home corner, raised right up, then press “Set this as home (0,0)”. The motors do not move. The bed picture marks that corner.

  2. Far corner. Move to the furthest corner the torch can safely reach.

  3. Lowest point. No sheet on. Lower the torch onto a slat (1 mm steps at the end) until the Floating head light comes on.

  4. Save. The machine then checks every move against these edges. After you save, the torch is raised and moved back to the home corner, so you can put it on the sheet's back-left and press Start here.

Motor direction

Each axis is Stock or Reversed. If a + jog moves the head the wrong way, tick Reversed for that axis. Restart the board afterwards — jog and cut stay locked until then, then we’ll do a short test (X right, Y towards you, Z up).

Inputs (live)

A light comes on when that input is active. Press each switch by hand to check it's wired right.

Floating headon when the torch is pushed up (touching)
E-stopon when pressed
X home switchon when triggered
Y home switchon when triggered
Z home switchon when triggered
Pause buttonon when pressed
Start / resume buttonon when pressed
Torch relay (output)on when the torch is firing

Nothing lights when you press a switch? Check its wiring and the controller pin list (Show pins below). Light stays on with nothing pressed? The input is inverted – fix it with the $5 / $6 / $14 settings.

Tests

Touch-off and torch tests. Touch-off test: the torch finds the sheet and stops at pierce height, no arc. Test torch: hold to fire over scrap, let go to stop. It turns off after 2 seconds and needs a fresh press.

Touch-start: no arc in air — this checks the relay and air only.

If the Bluetooth link drops while the torch is on, use the hardware E-stop. Reconnecting the app may not be enough.

The controller console is for the table's admin Show pins, Show settings, Send settings and the G-code console only appear when you are signed in as an admin.
