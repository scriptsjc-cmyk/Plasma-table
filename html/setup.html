Machine edges (crash protection)

PositionUnknown
EdgesNot set

  1. Find home. Press Home. No home switches? Move the torch to your marked home corner, raised right up, then press “Torch is at home corner”.

  2. Far corner. Move to the furthest corner the torch can safely reach.

  3. Lowest point. No sheet on. Lower the torch onto a slat (1 mm steps at the end) until the Floating head light comes on.

  4. Save. The machine then checks every move against these edges.

Inputs (live)

A light comes on when that input is active. Press each switch by hand to check it's wired right.

Floating headon when the torch is pushed up (touching)
E-stopon when pressed
X home switchon when triggered
Y home switchon when triggered
Z home switchon when triggered
Pause buttonon when pressed
Start / resume buttonon when pressed
Torch relay (output)on when the torch is firing

Nothing lights when you press a switch? Check its wiring and the controller pin list (Show pins below). Light stays on with nothing pressed? The input is inverted – fix it with the $5 / $6 / $14 settings.

Tests

Touch-off test: the torch finds the sheet and stops at pierce height, no arc. Torch test: fires the plasma for half a second over scrap.
