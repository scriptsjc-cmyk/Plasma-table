      Saved on the table.

      Now restart the controller so it joins your Wi-Fi: press the button below, or switch the table's controller off and on again.

      Then find it

      1. Wait about 20 seconds.
      2. On a phone or computer on , open http://fluidnc.local. If that doesn't open, find the table in your router's list of devices and use its number (like 192.168.1.50).
      3. Here, type that name or number into IP or hostname under FluidNC (Wi-Fi) and press Connect to FluidNC.

      Can't join (wrong password?) It makes its own Wi-Fi again, usually called FluidNC (password 12345678). Join that, or plug the USB cable back in, and try again.

        Settings

        The everyday settings for your table, each with a short explanation.Firmware tools (steps/mm, invert, raw $ settings) are under Firmware, after a warning. Those writes only go out when the machine is Idle and no job is running, and the current $$ list is backed up first.

        Make it yours

        These choices stay on this device, and each signed-in account on this browser keeps its own. They are not uploaded to the account (the server has nowhere to keep them yet).

        Machine and axes

        Bed size is the travel the torch may use. Invert an axis if it moves the wrong way. Firmware can push invert and travel to the controller.

        Speeds and acceleration

        Maximum rates (mm/min) and acceleration (mm/s²). These are the controller limits, not the cut speed for a material — that lives under More cut options on the cutting screen.

        Torch and THC

        Pierce height is how high the torch sits while the arc starts. Cut height is the running height. THC (torch height control by voltage) is only used if the controller has it.

        Pierce and cut

        These same values appear on the cutting screen under More cut options and are written into the G-code for each pierce. The selected material preset fills them automatically.

        Pierce height, cut height and delay are on Torch & THC. Cut speed is under More cut options on the cutting screen.

        Material library

        Admin-published presets are the defaults. Anything you save here stays on this device and overrides the default for that material and thickness. The Cut flow uses the selected preset.

        Homing and limits

        Use the Setup tab wizard to find home and teach the bed edges (crash protection). Pro firmware tools can change $20–$27 and $130–$132. Soft limits need a homing cycle and a correct bed size.

        I/O

        Live readout of the torch relay, probe/ohmic, limits and e-stop. Press a switch on the machine to confirm the wiring. The same lamps are on the Setup tab.

        Units and G-code dialect

        The table and drawings are in millimetres. You can still export a file for Mach3, FluidNC or GRBL. Mach3 files have no $ commands, M3/M5 for the torch, G4 P in seconds, and are wrapped in %.

        About

        The version and commit tell you which release this browser is running. If it differs from what you expect, press Check for a new version.

        Backup and restore

        Save the app settings and your material presets, and (when connected) the controller $ list. Restore validates a $ backup before sending anything.
